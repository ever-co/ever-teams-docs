# Goals

A goal sets a number of hours to reach, or not to pass, in a day, a week or a month. Ever Teams measures it from the time tracked: "at least 30 hours a week on the client's project", or "at most 10 hours a week on the internal project".

Open **My work > Goals** (`/goals`).

:::note
The **Goals** entry shows only when the server offers time goals. On a server without them, the page says "Goals are not available".
:::

## Creating a goal

Press **New goal** and fill in:

| Field | What it sets |
| ----- | ------------ |
| Goal name | How the goal is listed |
| Rule | **At least** (a target to reach) or **At most** (a limit not to pass) |
| Hours | The number of hours, with up to two decimals |
| Period | **Per day**, **Per week** or **Per month** |
| Projects | The projects whose time counts; **Any project** counts them all |
| Clients | The clients whose time counts; **Any client** counts them all |
| Billable only | Count billable time only |

A goal cannot ask for more hours than its period holds: 24 a day, 168 a week, 744 a month.

A goal has no start or end date. It applies to each period, one after the other, until it is archived.

## Following a goal

Each goal shows the time tracked against its target, the percentage, and what is left: the time **to go**, **Target reached**, or the time **over the limit**. A badge says where it stands: **In progress**, **Achieved**, **On track** or **Exceeded**.

Switch between **Today**, **This week** and **This month** to see the goals of that period. Progress is counted in each member's own time zone and refreshed every minute while the page is open.

The goals of the week also show on the **My week** page, with a link to all goals.

## Changing a goal

From a goal's menu:

- **Edit goal** changes its name, rule, hours, period, projects, clients and the billable switch. Who the goal is for cannot be changed afterwards.
- **Archive goal** stops it, with **Undo** for a moment. Archived goals stay in an **Archived goals** section, where **Restore** brings one back.

A goal is archived, never deleted.

## Goals for a team

A team manager also sees **Members of your team**, with each member's goals, for the team they have open. When creating a goal, a manager chooses who it is for:

- **Me**
- **One member** of the team
- **Whole team**

A person can change their own goals. A manager can change the goals of the members of the teams they manage, and those teams' goals.
